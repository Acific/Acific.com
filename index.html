§03 / PROTOCOL

Method

  1. STEP 01

    Observe

    Learn your goals, users and constraints. Define what a successful result looks like.

  2. STEP 02

    Hypothesize

    Plan the architecture and design the experience before any production code.

  3. STEP 03

    Iterate

    Short cycles with frequent demos. You see the specimen evolve every week.

  4. STEP 04

    Release

    Ship, measure, keep improving. We stay in the lab after launch.
