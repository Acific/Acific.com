Hypothesis

AI should do real work, not demos. The most useful assistants will be the quiet ones: wired into the tools people already use, taking the boring, repetitive parts off their plate.

What we're exploring

  • Assistants that work inside existing tools instead of beside them
  • Pipelines that turn messy, scattered data into something usable
  • Workflows that automate repetitive steps end to end

Status

In development. Nothing public yet. If you'd like early access or want to collaborate, get in touch.
