About

An independent software lab

Acific prototypes its own products and AI tools, and runs commissioned experiments for teams who want something that doesn't exist yet.

Protocol

  1. 01ObserveGoals, users, constraints. Define what success looks like.
  2. 02HypothesizeArchitecture and experience, designed before production code.
  3. 03IterateShort cycles and frequent demos, so you watch it evolve weekly.
  4. 04ReleaseShip, measure, keep improving. We stay in the lab after launch.
