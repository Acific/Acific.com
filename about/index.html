About

Acific is an independent software lab. We prototype our own products and AI tools, and run commissioned experiments for teams who want something that doesn't exist yet.

Protocol

  1. 01Observe

    Goals, users, constraints. Define what success looks like.

  2. 02Hypothesize

    Architecture and experience, designed before production code.

  3. 03Iterate

    Short cycles, frequent demos. Watch the specimen evolve weekly.

  4. 04Release

    Ship, measure, keep improving. We stay in the lab after launch.
